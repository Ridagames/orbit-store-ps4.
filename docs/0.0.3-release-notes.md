**Stream & install from Orbit Zero, plus review scores and playtimes.**

## Orbit Store · PS4

- **Review scores and playtimes.** 433 PS4 games now show their Metacritic score and 742 show HowLongToBeat playtimes. Filter games by score or playtime, sort by them, and choose Main Story, Main + Extras or Completionist. Show them on game cards with **Scores and playtimes on game cards** in Appearance.
- **More download choices.** Orbit now has **1,434 games with 1,551 download options**, plus **533 add-ons with 535 options across 120 base games**.
- **Firmware 13.52 and 14.00.** Orbit now includes newer versions of elfldr and the payload’s startup code that support these firmware versions.
- **Always use TorBox.** Orbit remembers whether you last chose Direct or TorBox. Turn on **Always use TorBox** in TorBox settings to use TorBox for every new download.
- **A smoother TV app.** Covers load faster, Browse opens without a pause, and the app no longer waits more than a minute to respond when the computer running Orbit Zero is off or has moved to a new address.
- Other improvements and bug fixes.

## Orbit Zero · Mac, Windows and Linux

- **Stream & install on PS4.** Install a game on your PS4 straight from your computer, without copying it to the PS4’s drive first. Needs Orbit Store 0.0.3 on your PS4.
- **Starts Orbit on firmware 13.52 and 14.00.** Orbit Zero 1.9.2’s **Start Orbit on PS4** sends the newer elfldr.
- **TorBox improvements.** Zero now tells you when TorBox can’t get a file instead of waiting forever, **Always use TorBox** no longer blocks AkiraBox downloads, and **Download via TorBox** no longer greys out after Zero has been open for a while.

## What you need

A PS4 with GoldHEN. Turn on GoldHEN’s payload loader once, in **GoldHEN → Servers Settings → Enable PayLoader Server**, so Orbit can start. For Orbit Zero, a Mac, Windows or Linux computer on the same home network as your PS4, over Wi-Fi or Ethernet.

## Install or update the native TV app (PKG)

Download `orbit_store_ps4.pkg` from **Assets**, verify its `.sha256`, and copy it to your PS4 on a USB drive. Install it with GoldHEN’s **Settings → Debug Settings → Package Installer**, then open **Orbit Store** from the Games row. Native app **0.0.3** includes the matching Orbit Store **0.0.3** service and elfldr.

Close other apps such as ezRemote before opening Orbit. With them open, the PS4 may not have enough memory left to start it.

## Use Orbit Zero with your PS4

Download the `Orbit-Zero-1.9.2-*` package for your computer from **Assets**: macOS DMG, Windows installer or ZIP, or Ubuntu/Debian DEB, for ARM64 or x64. Open Orbit on your PS4, then in Orbit Zero go to **Settings → Devices**, enter your PS4’s IP address, select **Connect to Orbit** and enter the code shown on your PS4. [Step-by-step guide](https://github.com/saawant12/orbit-store-ps4/blob/main/guides/orbit-zero.md)

Orbit Zero 1.9.2 works with your PS5 too. Handing Vikingfile and AkiraBox links to a PS5 needs Orbit for PS5 1.9.1 or later.

## Use the browser version (ELF)

Download `orbit_store.elf` and its `.sha256` from **Assets**, verify the checksum, then send the ELF once to an ELF loader on port 9021, such as elfldr. GoldHEN’s own payload loader on port 9090 can’t run it; it’s too big. Orbit saves its service and elfldr, and adds the **Orbit Store Browser** icon. Open it, or `http://<ps4-ip>:34177/` on another device on the same local network.

To start Orbit when the PS4 turns on, see [Payload manager and auto-start](https://github.com/saawant12/orbit-store-ps4/blob/main/guides/getting-started.md#payload-manager-and-auto-start).

## Good to know

- Right after the PS4 turns on, Orbit sometimes needs a second try to start. The app and the auto-start copy try again by themselves, so the first start can take up to about 40 seconds longer.
- **Without Orbit Zero, Vikingfile and AkiraBox still take a few minutes.** On PS4, the Cloudflare check on their pages can take 2–3 minutes to pass. Leave the page open: once the check passes, the download works as usual. With a Vikingfile Premium account [signed in to Orbit](https://github.com/saawant12/orbit-store-ps4/blob/main/guides/vikingfile.md), there’s usually no wait.
- AkiraBox may show an ad instead of the download. Close it and try again, or choose another source for the game.
- Some packages can’t be installed straight from your computer. Orbit Zero then tells you to send them to the PS4 instead.
- Orbit Zero uses one console at a time. Finish or cancel the transfers to one console before switching to the other.
- Scores and playtimes come from published Metacritic and HowLongToBeat data. Games without them show none; playtimes are community estimates and vary by player.

## Downloads and source

- **PS4:** `orbit_store_ps4.pkg` includes the native TV app, the download service and elfldr. Use `orbit_store.elf` to run the service and browser interface on their own.
- **Mac, Windows and Linux:** choose the `Orbit-Zero-1.9.2-*` package for your operating system and processor. Orbit Zero includes Orbit for PS5 1.9.2, whose source code is in `orbit-store-1.9.2-source-bundle.zip` in this release’s **Assets**, and elfldr a481740, whose source code is in `orbit-store-0.0.3-source-bundle.zip`.
- **Source code:** `orbit-store-0.0.3-source-bundle.zip` includes the Store source code, dependencies, elfldr’s source, licence notices and build instructions.
- **Checksums:** use `SHA256SUMS` to check your download, or run `shasum -a 256 -c <filename>.sha256` for the ELF or PKG.
