# Orbit Store 0.0.1 for PS4 (Beta)

**Orbit Store is now on PS4,** with a native app on your Games row and the browser version for your phone or computer.

- **A native app on your PS4.** Browse **1,110 PS4 games with 1,227 download options**, plus **533 add-ons with 535 options across 120 base games**. Choose the Store, Crossbar, Cinema or Panorama layout, in 26 languages.
- **Nothing to start first.** The app brings its own ELF loader, elfldr, and starts Orbit’s download service by itself through GoldHEN’s payload loader. GoldHEN AutoRun can start Orbit when the PS4 turns on, and LaunchPad any time.
- **Downloads that keep going.** Download from Archive.org, Vikingfile and AkiraBox, or through TorBox. Pause and resume, carry on after a restart, unpack ZIP, 7z and RAR archives, then install the game on your PS4. Downloads keep running when you close the app.
- **Library and Files.** See your installed games, and browse the internal drive and USB drives to copy, move, rename, delete, unpack or install.
- **Use your phone or computer.** Pair it at `http://<ps4-ip>:34177/` and use the browser version, with the same queue, sources and settings as the TV app.

> **Orbit stays free.** [Orbit Store](https://github.com/saawant12/orbit-store-ps4), [Orbit Zero](https://github.com/saawant12/orbit-store-ps5#orbit-zero-for-mac-windows-and-linux), [Orbit Play](https://github.com/saawant12/orbit-play-ps5) and our upcoming apps are free and will stay free.

## What you need

A PS4 with GoldHEN. Turn on GoldHEN’s payload loader once, in **GoldHEN → Servers Settings → Enable PayLoader Server**, so Orbit can start. This release was tested on a PS4 on firmware 12.00 with GoldHEN.

## Install the native TV app (PKG)

Download `orbit_store_ps4.pkg` from **Assets**, verify its `.sha256`, and copy it to your PS4 on a USB drive. Install it with GoldHEN’s **Settings → Debug Settings → Package Installer**, then open **Orbit Store** from the Games row. Native app **0.0.1** includes the matching Orbit Store **0.0.1** service and elfldr.

Close other apps such as ezRemote before opening Orbit. With them open, the PS4 may not have enough memory left to start it.

## Use the browser version (ELF)

Download `orbit_store.elf` and its `.sha256` from **Assets**, verify the checksum, then send the ELF once to an ELF loader on port 9021, such as elfldr. GoldHEN’s own payload loader on port 9090 can’t run it; it’s too big. Orbit saves its service and elfldr, and adds the **Orbit Store Browser** icon. Open it, or `http://<ps4-ip>:34177/` on another device on the same local network.

To start Orbit when the PS4 turns on, see [Payload manager and auto-start](https://github.com/saawant12/orbit-store-ps4/blob/main/guides/getting-started.md#payload-manager-and-auto-start).

## Good to know

- Right after the PS4 turns on, Orbit sometimes needs a second try to start. The app and the auto-start copy try again by themselves, so the first start can take up to about 40 seconds longer.
- **Vikingfile and AkiraBox take a few minutes for now.** On PS4, the Cloudflare check on their pages can take 2–3 minutes to pass. Leave the page open: once the check passes, the download works as usual. We’re working on making this faster.
- AkiraBox may show an ad instead of the download. Close it and try again, or choose another source for the game.

## Downloads and source

- **PS4:** `orbit_store_ps4.pkg` includes the native TV app, the download service and elfldr. Use `orbit_store.elf` to run the service and browser interface on their own.
- **Source code:** `orbit-store-0.0.1-source-bundle.zip` includes the Store source code, dependencies, elfldr’s source, licence notices and build instructions.
- **Checksums:** use `SHA256SUMS` to check your download, or run `shasum -a 256 -c <filename>.sha256` for the ELF or PKG.
