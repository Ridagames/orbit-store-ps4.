# Orbit Store 0.0.2 (Beta)

**A big update: Orbit Zero now works with your PS4.**

**With Orbit Zero on your computer**

- **Download on your computer, play on your PS4.** Orbit Zero for Mac, Windows and Linux downloads PS4 games on your computer, sends them to your PS4 over your home network and installs them. Your PS4 doesn’t need internet access for these downloads.
- **No more Cloudflare wait.** With Orbit Zero, Vikingfile and AkiraBox pages open on your computer, where their check passes in seconds. Zero then downloads the game and sends it to your PS4. With **Download preference** set to **Prefer PS4** in Zero’s **Settings → Downloads**, Zero hands the download link to your PS4 instead, which downloads the game itself. The PS4 app points to Orbit Zero on those pages.
- **Start Orbit from your computer.** In Orbit Zero, open **Settings → Updates & startup**, choose **PS4** and select **Start Orbit on PS4**. Zero sends Orbit to your PS4’s ELF loader, and starts elfldr through GoldHEN first when needed.
- **One Orbit Zero for PS5 and PS4.** Each console gets its own profile, with its own pairing, games and downloads. Switch between them in one click.

**Also new in Orbit Store on your PS4**

- The catalogue now has **1,434 PS4 games and 1,551 download options**, plus **533 add-ons with 535 options across 120 base games**.
- **Sign in to Vikingfile from another device.** If Vikingfile’s sign-in page freezes on your PS4, sign in on a computer and paste the sign-in pass into Orbit. [How to copy the pass](https://github.com/saawant12/orbit-store-ps4/blob/main/guides/vikingfile.md#use-a-pass-from-another-device)
- **Starts more reliably at boot.** When GoldHEN AutoRun starts Orbit while the network is still coming up, Orbit keeps trying for longer instead of giving up.
- **Orbit stays reachable after a network drop.** If your PS4’s Wi-Fi drops for a moment, Orbit now opens its connection again instead of staying unreachable until you restart it.
- Other improvements and bug fixes.

> **Orbit stays free.** [Orbit Store](https://github.com/saawant12/orbit-store-ps4), [Orbit Zero](https://github.com/saawant12/orbit-store-ps5#orbit-zero-for-mac-windows-and-linux), [Orbit Play](https://github.com/saawant12/orbit-play-ps5) and our upcoming apps are free and will stay free.

## What you need

A PS4 with GoldHEN. Turn on GoldHEN’s payload loader once, in **GoldHEN → Servers Settings → Enable PayLoader Server**, so Orbit can start. For Orbit Zero, a Mac, Windows or Linux computer on the same home network as your PS4, over Wi-Fi or Ethernet.

## Install or update the native TV app (PKG)

Download `orbit_store_ps4.pkg` from **Assets**, verify its `.sha256`, and copy it to your PS4 on a USB drive. Install it with GoldHEN’s **Settings → Debug Settings → Package Installer**, then open **Orbit Store** from the Games row. Native app **0.0.2** includes the matching Orbit Store **0.0.2** service and elfldr.

Close other apps such as ezRemote before opening Orbit. With them open, the PS4 may not have enough memory left to start it.

## Use Orbit Zero with your PS4

Download the `Orbit-Zero-1.9.1-*` package for your computer from **Assets**: macOS DMG, Windows installer or ZIP, or Ubuntu/Debian DEB, for ARM64 or x64. Open Orbit on your PS4, then in Orbit Zero go to **Settings → Devices**, enter your PS4’s IP address, select **Connect to Orbit** and enter the code shown on your PS4. [Step-by-step guide](https://github.com/saawant12/orbit-store-ps4/blob/main/guides/orbit-zero.md)

Orbit Zero 1.9.1 works with your PS5 too. Handing Vikingfile and AkiraBox links to a PS5 needs Orbit for PS5 1.9.1 or later.

## Use the browser version (ELF)

Download `orbit_store.elf` and its `.sha256` from **Assets**, verify the checksum, then send the ELF once to an ELF loader on port 9021, such as elfldr. GoldHEN’s own payload loader on port 9090 can’t run it; it’s too big. Orbit saves its service and elfldr, and adds the **Orbit Store Browser** icon. Open it, or `http://<ps4-ip>:34177/` on another device on the same local network.

To start Orbit when the PS4 turns on, see [Payload manager and auto-start](https://github.com/saawant12/orbit-store-ps4/blob/main/guides/getting-started.md#payload-manager-and-auto-start).

## Good to know

- Right after the PS4 turns on, Orbit sometimes needs a second try to start. The app and the auto-start copy try again by themselves, so the first start can take up to about 40 seconds longer.
- **Without Orbit Zero, Vikingfile and AkiraBox still take a few minutes.** On PS4, the Cloudflare check on their pages can take 2–3 minutes to pass. Leave the page open: once the check passes, the download works as usual. With a Vikingfile Premium account [signed in to Orbit](https://github.com/saawant12/orbit-store-ps4/blob/main/guides/vikingfile.md), there’s usually no wait.
- AkiraBox may show an ad instead of the download. Close it and try again, or choose another source for the game.
- Orbit Zero uses one console at a time. Finish or cancel the transfers to one console before switching to the other.
- Orbit Zero’s **Stream & install** isn’t available for PS4 yet. Zero sends the game to your PS4’s drive and installs it from there.

## Downloads and source

- **PS4:** `orbit_store_ps4.pkg` includes the native TV app, the download service and elfldr. Use `orbit_store.elf` to run the service and browser interface on their own.
- **Mac, Windows and Linux:** choose the `Orbit-Zero-1.9.1-*` package for your operating system and processor. Orbit Zero includes Orbit for PS5 1.9.0, whose source code is in `orbit-store-1.9.0-source-bundle.zip` on the [Orbit for PS5 1.9.0 release](https://github.com/saawant12/orbit-store-ps5/releases/tag/v1.9.0), and elfldr 0.7, whose source code is in this release’s source bundle.
- **Source code:** `orbit-store-0.0.2-source-bundle.zip` includes the Store source code, dependencies, elfldr’s source, licence notices and build instructions.
- **Checksums:** use `SHA256SUMS` to check your download, or run `shasum -a 256 -c <filename>.sha256` for the ELF or PKG.
